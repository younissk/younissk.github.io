An early attempt at the fitness app that eventually became Saha Fit. Routines are assembled from an
exercise list through a multi-step flow — select exercises, arrange them into a routine, save — on
top of a hand-rolled set of styled primitives (button, card, input, text, FAB) and a custom bottom
navigation.
