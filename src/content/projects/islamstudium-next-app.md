An early take on a learning platform for Islamic studies: session-based auth wired through a NextAuth provider at the layout root, a courses catalogue, a `learn` route for working through lesson content, and user account pages, all on the Next.js App Router with API routes for the backend.

Worth reading as the precursor to the much larger LMS work that followed — the domain modelling here is what later got rebuilt properly.
