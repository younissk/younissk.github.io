A course-video platform built as a demo: browse a grid of videos pulled from a Supabase table, open a detail page to watch, sign in through a demo account, and pick between subscription tiers with a mock Stripe checkout. Route protection, auth context and the Stripe client/checkout split are the parts with actual structure; the rest is Mantine UI over a small schema.
