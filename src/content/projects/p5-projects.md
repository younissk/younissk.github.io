A dozen or so browser sketches, each self-contained and published to GitHub Pages from an index page that links them all. They move through the usual creative-coding syllabus — sine waves, vectors, oscillation — into gravitational attraction and particle systems, then into pure maths visualisations: the Mandelbrot set, Maurer roses, Bézier curves, metaballs, snowflake generation, self-avoiding walks and a cloth-wave simulation.
