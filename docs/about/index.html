About

About

I'm an AI engineer at ONDEWO, working on AI4Emergency. Before I wrote software for a field, I worked in the field.

  • Last updated: 2026-08

Health care

Samariterbund · 9 months

I worked as a paramedic at Samariterbund for nine months. Emergency response, patients in front of me, and a job where the time something takes is part of whether it works.

Construction

HTL Mödling · ib-data / ABK

I spent five years at HTL Mödling on the construction track, then three years at ib-data building software for construction management. Semantic search over specifications, PDF and Excel sources turned into structured data, a customer portal, a detection model for doors and windows in drawings.

Artificial intelligence

JKU Linz · ONDEWO

I did my BSc in Artificial Intelligence at JKU Linz and finished ahead of the standard duration, with a thesis on deep learning for language-based audio retrieval. I now work at ONDEWO on retrieval, evaluation and agent infrastructure in Python.

What it means for the work

AI4Emergency is AI for emergency communication, and I have worked in emergency response. The document work at ib-data was construction documents, and I was trained in construction before that. In both cases I knew what the documents were for and who was reading them before I started building.

That does not make a model more accurate on its own. It does mean I can usually tell when an output is wrong.
